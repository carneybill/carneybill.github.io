Content and products are cheap. Credibility has to be earned.

VeriTrust puts the premise of my book, B2B Marketing in the AI Era, to work on your website. As AI makes content and digital products cheaper to produce, publishing more or shipping another feature becomes less of a distinction. Credibility and authority matter more: why should a buyer believe you, choose you, or trust your judgment?

Your website should help them answer that. Clear positioning tells buyers who you help and where you fit. Specific results give them something to verify. Honest constraints show where your product stops being the right choice. Together, those signals give buyers a basis for trusting your expertise.

VeriTrust checks the published evidence behind those signals and shows what needs work. More pages do not earn more points. A score is a diagnostic, not a certification of your company or a guarantee that buyers or AI systems will recommend you.

The problem

Buyers and AI systems no longer take a website's claims at face value. They check what is actually published: titles, descriptions, structured data, trust and pricing pages, working links, and proof. Most sites have never been audited against that standard, so nobody actually knows what is missing.

Who it is for

B2B companies who want an honest, evidence-backed read on what their website currently proves, not a subjective design opinion, before deciding what to fix.
