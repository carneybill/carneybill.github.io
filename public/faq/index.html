What does Carney do?

Carney helps technical B2B companies clarify positioning, improve buyer-facing proof, build founder-led content systems, make AI platform decisions, and operate supervised paid-media workflows.

How do I choose the right offer?

Start with the decision that is blocked. Use the Positioning + Pipeline Audit for diagnosis, Proof Ops for evidence gaps, Founder Content Engine for recurring founder-led publishing, the AI Platform Owner's Rep for platform decisions, and the PPC Performance Agent for supervised paid-media operations.

Is pricing public?

Yes. Current public prices are listed on the Pricing page and on each detailed offer page. The detailed offer and checkout amount control before purchase.

Does AI replace Bill's work?

No. AI can support research, analysis, drafting, code, and quality checks. Bill retains responsibility for strategy, judgment, claim verification, and final quality.

Will client information become public proof?

Not without permission. Client names, results, internal documents, and private evidence are not published as proof unless the client has approved that use.

What happens before an engagement starts?

The decision, scope, owner, source material, review path, timing, and evidence permissions are defined before substantive work begins. The implementation page explains the full operating path.
