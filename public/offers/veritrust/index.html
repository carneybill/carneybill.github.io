The problem

Buyers and AI systems no longer take a website's claims at face value. They check what is actually published: titles, descriptions, structured data, trust and pricing pages, working links, and proof. Most sites have never been audited against that standard, so nobody actually knows what is missing.

Who it is for

B2B companies who want an honest, evidence-backed read on what their website currently proves, not a subjective design opinion, before deciding what to fix.

What's included

Baseline VeriTrust report

A scored audit across positioning, verification depth, proof ops, discovery, credibility, path friction, mechanics, and freshness, with every finding tied to the published evidence that triggered it.

Tracked re-checks

Ongoing audits compared against your original baseline, so score movement reflects real, published change, not opinion.

Private delivery

Your report lives in your own client dashboard. Nothing about your site or your score is published publicly.

Deterministic scoring

Findings are structural and reproducible. A dimension without enough evidence is marked unscored instead of guessed.

How it works

  1. Baseline: VeriTrust crawls your published site and produces your first scored report.
  2. Fix: you, or I, address what the report surfaces.
  3. Re-check: a new run compares against your original baseline, so movement is traceable to evidence, not guesswork.
